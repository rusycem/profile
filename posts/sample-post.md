## Hello World!

Welcome to my new portfolio and blog. I decided to revamp the entire site into a **Game Engine Interface** because I wanted something that truly reflects my passion for game development. 

### Why this design?
As game developers, we spend thousands of hours staring at dark, technical interfaces like Unreal Engine, Unity, and Visual Studio. I thought it would be a fun and memorable way to present my projects as "Assets" in a Content Browser.

### What to expect here
I plan to write about:
* Behind-the-scenes looks at my Unreal Engine 5 projects
* Programming patterns in C++ and C#
* Tutorials and tips I pick up along the way
